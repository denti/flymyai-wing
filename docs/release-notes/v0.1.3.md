**Fixes a false warning that appeared on every Mac, on every launch.**

### The bug

v0.1.1 and v0.1.2 greeted you with:

> Another app is already keeping this Mac awake: powerd (pid 368).

`powerd` is Apple's own power-management daemon. Its assertion is held whenever the display is
on — which is to say always, on every Mac — and it does not interfere with Lidwing in any way.
The message was false, alarming, and called the operating system an app. It was also the first
thing a new user ever saw.

The detector was asking the wrong question. It asked *who is preventing sleep*; what matters is
*who will interfere with Lidwing*. Lidwing blocks the lid-close sleep specifically, and the
common assertions — `caffeinate`, Electron apps, Handoff, Spotlight indexing, trackpad activity —
are a different mechanism entirely. Lidwing coexists with all of them, so it now says nothing
about any of them.

What remains: if something is holding your Mac awake outright, such as Internet Sharing, you get
one quiet line in the menu saying so. And if the lid-close setting is already switched on by
something that is not Lidwing, that is a real conflict and it is offered for repair — without a
dialog on the launch path, which is what crashed v0.1.0.

### Fewer messages, on purpose

Every notification, alert and badge in this app now has to answer one question: **what do you do
differently because of it?** Three failed and were deleted rather than reworded:

- the greeting when Lidwing first turns on — you have just installed a menu-bar app and are
  looking at the menu bar;
- "macOS changed and Lidwing still works" — true, and nothing to act on. Still recorded in the
  logs, where it explains a failure if one ever comes;
- the pop-up on first launch pointing at the menu bar.

The icon carries the state silently. Text interrupts only when there is something to do about it.

**The chime when you close the lid stays**, and is exempt from all of the above: it is the one
signal that reaches you when you cannot see the screen, which is the entire point of the app.

### Unchanged, and still the important part

The mechanism is proven on **one** Mac for **twelve seconds**, with a positive control. No 8-hour
soak, no evidence about charger and display events, nothing on Intel, Air, fanless, T2, or any
macOS other than 15.5. The app tells you when it is running on hardware nobody has tested it on.

**276 tests on Linux, 281 on macOS 15 and macOS 26**, zero failures, zero skipped.
